PHILOSOPHY

INECHOS isn’t made to chase everyone—it is a choice, not forced. It exists for those who deliberately choose the utmost quality

INECHOS started in 2026 while I was doing my A-Levels. Between studying and late nights in the kitchen, I realized two things: the people around me kept coming back for what I was baking, and I could never find a cookie in a café or on a store shelf that actually felt worth eating. Most were mass-produced, overly sweet, or built on cheap substitutes.
So I stopped looking and started perfecting. I stripped the recipe down to its fundamentals and obsessed over every variable—browning the butter, testing ratios, and sourcing only the highest-grade raw materials available on the market.
INECHOS isn’t trying to reinvent the cookie. It began in 2026 with a simple rule: take a classic and refuse to cut a single corner.
