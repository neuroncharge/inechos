METHOD

01 / OUR STORE — PICKUP

We don’t have an official INECHOS storefront yet. Instead, you can visit Noor Al Sabah Cafeteria in Ajman to collect your order, or come by for a complimentary cookie to taste before you decide. Because everything is baked fresh in small runs, make sure to message us before you head over so we can have one baked and waiting for you.

02 / ORDER — TIME

Nothing at INECHOS is pre-baked or sitting on a shelf. Please place your order at least 12 to 24 hours before your expected delivery time so we can prepare your batch fresh from scratch. Use the Order Now button to select your items from our WhatsApp catalog, and we’ll confirm your order directly.

03 / CADENCE — DELIVERY LOCATION

We currently deliver exclusively across Ajman and Sharjah, bringing every batch straight from the oven to your door.
Delivery in Ajman is complimentary on orders above 50 AED, with a 10 AED fee for orders below that amount. For Sharjah, delivery is complimentary on orders above 100 AED, or 20 AED for orders under 100 AED.
